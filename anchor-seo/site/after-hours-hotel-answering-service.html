After-hours hotel answering service for calls that cannot wait.

Anchor helps hotels and motels answer overnight calls, capture booking demand, support late-arrival questions, and route urgent guest issues without adding another overnight employee.

Best fit: owner-operated hotels, motels, extended-stay properties, and limited-service properties that miss calls overnight or during thinly staffed shifts.

Why after-hours calls matter

After-hours callers often have immediate intent. They may need a room tonight, have a late-arrival question, want to confirm pet policy, need directions, or be comparing hotels before booking. If the call goes to voicemail, the next property often wins.

What Anchor can answer overnight

  • Availability and starting-rate guidance from approved property information.
  • Late check-in, breakfast, parking, pet policy, and local questions.
  • Group, crew, wedding, funeral, and sports-team inquiry capture.
  • Spanish-language guest questions and simple routing.
  • Urgent issues that should be transferred or escalated to staff.

How it protects credibility

Anchor is designed to avoid overpromising. Ava answers from approved property facts, captures the caller's details, and escalates uncertain calls instead of pretending to confirm rates, exceptions, or manager decisions.

Cost comparison
