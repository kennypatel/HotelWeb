Anchor AI Summary

Anchor is a hotel AI phone agent for limited-service hotels. It answers calls around the clock, captures booking intent, identifies group and long-stay leads, handles common guest questions, and routes important inquiries to hotel staff.

Short Description

Anchor is AI-powered phone support for hotel operations. It is built by hotel owner-operators for limited-service properties that lose revenue when phones go unanswered after hours or when front desk staff are helping in-person guests.

What Anchor Does

Anchor helps hotels avoid missed revenue from unanswered calls. A caller asking about tonight's availability, a youth sports block, a long-stay project crew, breakfast hours, pet policy, parking, or late arrival can get a fast answer or be routed into a clean follow-up workflow. The system is designed to capture useful details and notify the right hotel contact rather than guess at sensitive answers.

What Anchor Is Not

Anchor is not a booking engine, property management system, generic chatbot, or broad AI agency. It is a focused hotel phone agent for limited-service hotel operations. It does not need to quote final confirmed rates or modify PMS records to be useful.
