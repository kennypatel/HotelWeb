Overflow-on-no-answer routing

Setup is a forwarding rule, not a phone-system replacement: if the desk doesn't answer by the third ring, the call goes to Ava. She answers from your approved factsheet, captures booking details, and transfers anything urgent back to staff. When the desk is free, nothing changes — your team still answers first.

FAQs

What is hotel front desk overflow?

Front desk overflow happens when guest calls arrive while staff are busy with check-ins, in-person guests, property tasks, breakfast, or night audit work.

How does Anchor help with front desk overflow?

Anchor answers calls, handles approved questions, captures booking or group lead details, and escalates calls that need staff attention.

How does overflow-on-no-answer routing work?

Your phone system forwards any call your desk doesn't pick up within a set number of rings — commonly three — to Ava. Staffed moments stay exactly as they are; only the calls that would have rung out get answered.

What stays with my team?

In-person guests, confirmed bookings, final rates, sensitive situations, and anything you define as escalation-worthy. Ava handles volume and capture; your team keeps judgment and decisions.

What reporting do we get?

Monthly reporting shows overflow call volume by hour, what was answered, captured booking and group leads, and transfers — so you can see exactly what the desk couldn't reach.
