An AI receptionist for hotels and motels.

Anchor is a hotel-specific AI receptionist that answers guest calls, captures revenue opportunities, supports routine questions, and routes important calls to the right person.

Positioning: Anchor is not a website chatbot. It is a voice-first AI phone agent for hotel and motel calls.

What makes a hotel AI receptionist different

Hotels have different phone workflows than ordinary offices. A caller may need a room tonight, a group block, a weekly stay, a pet-policy answer, directions, or a late-arrival solution. A hotel AI receptionist needs to know what it can answer and when to escalate.

What Anchor can do

  • Answer approved guest questions in a consistent voice.
  • Capture booking, group, crew, wedding, and long-stay intent.
  • Support English and Spanish call handling.
  • Reduce repetitive front desk interruptions.
  • Route urgent or uncertain calls to staff.

Why owner-operators use it

Owner-operated properties need practical coverage that does not create a large IT project. Anchor can work from a property factsheet and phone routing, making it easier to test before committing to deeper integrations.
