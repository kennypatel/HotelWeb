AI phone service for hotels that cannot afford to miss calls.

Anchor is an AI phone service for hotels, built for limited-service hotel owner-operators who lose bookings when the front desk is busy or closed. It answers calls 24/7, captures the guest's request, and routes the important details to the right hotel contact.

What the service handles

Anchor can answer common hotel questions, capture after-hours booking inquiries, identify group and corporate leads, route long-stay requests, support English and Spanish callers, and send owner alerts when a call looks valuable. It is useful for front desk overflow, late-night calls, and small teams that cannot keep a dedicated phone operator on every shift.

Why hotels use it

Hotel calls are often high intent. Someone calling about tonight's availability, a youth sports block, a wedding group, a traveling nurse stay, or a crew rate is close to spending money. If nobody answers, that demand usually goes to the next property. Anchor keeps the conversation alive and gives staff a clean follow-up path.

No PMS integration required

Anchor works on top of the existing hotel phone line. It does not need direct access to the property management system to answer FAQs, collect booking intent, or route a lead. That makes it easier for hotels to launch quickly without waiting on a franchise approval process.

Best fit: limited-service hotels, owner-operated properties, and small hotel portfolios that need reliable call answering without adding overnight headcount.
