A hotel AI agent for the calls that turn into revenue.

Anchor is a hotel AI agent for limited-service properties. It answers phone calls, handles common guest questions, captures structured booking details, and routes valuable leads to owners, managers, or front desk teams.

What a hotel AI agent should do

A useful hotel AI agent should not simply talk. It should know when to answer, when to collect details, when to escalate, and when to avoid overpromising. Anchor is designed to capture intent and keep staff in control of confirmed rates, sensitive issues, and final booking decisions.

Calls Anchor can handle

Anchor can help with late-night availability questions, group blocks, wedding blocks, youth sports teams, long-stay project crews, traveling nurses, breakfast hours, pet policies, parking, directions, Spanish-speaking guests, and front desk overflow during busy shifts.

Why hotels choose Anchor

Anchor does not require a direct PMS integration to start helping. It uses a property factsheet, answers from approved operating details, and escalates high-value or uncertain calls. That keeps implementation practical for owner-operated hotels and small portfolios.
