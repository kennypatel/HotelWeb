Phone Coverage for Independent Hotels

Franchised properties lean on an 800-number that answers when the desk can't. Independents get whatever the desk can manage — and after the lobby closes, that's usually voicemail. The result is a structural disadvantage that has nothing to do with the quality of your property: the brands answer more of their calls than you do.

Anchor closes that gap. Ava gives independent hotels what the flags built for themselves — 24/7 answering, booking capture, and escalation — except trained on your property specifically and priced for a single owner, not a system.

The CRS gap

Central reservations is the quiet advantage of franchising: a caller who can't reach the desk still reaches the brand, and the booking still lands. The independent equivalent has always been ugly — voicemail, the owner's cell, or a generic answering service that takes messages about perishable bookings. Ava is the missing piece: an agent that knows your property, answers instantly on every ring, and captures the booking with structured details instead of a message.

What independents miss after the lobby closes

The after-close call mix is disproportionately revenue: same-night travelers comparing whoever answers, group organizers planning late, tomorrow's arrivals with check-in questions. A closed lobby with a live phone line is a property that's still selling; a closed lobby with voicemail is closed, period. Owner-operator setup, no PMS needed

Independent owners are rightly wary of integration projects — most have one tech stack and no IT department. Anchor sidesteps the problem: there is no PMS integration required. You complete a factsheet with your rate guidance, amenities, policies, and escalation contacts; we verify everything on a test line; you forward calls. Your property's voice on the phone is configured in days, and changing an answer is editing a document, not filing a ticket.

Pricing for single properties

One setup fee, one flat monthly retainer. No per-minute meter, no multi-property minimums, no annual contract designed for a portfolio. For most independents, the comparison isn't against another service — it's against one fully loaded desk shift, or one missed group block a quarter. Independent hotel phone coverage FAQs

How is this different from a brand's central reservations?

A CRS answers for the flag, on the flag's terms. Anchor answers for your property specifically — your rates guidance, your policies, your escalation contacts — which is the coverage independents never had access to.

Does setup require a PMS or channel manager project?

No. Ava runs from an approved property factsheet on your existing phone line. Most independent properties go live within days.

Is pricing viable for a single property?

Yes — one setup fee and one flat monthly retainer per property, sized for single-property owners rather than portfolios. No per-minute billing.

Who answers when something urgent happens?

Your people. Emergencies, lockouts, and in-stay issues transfer immediately to the on-call contact you designate, under rules you test before launch.

Can Ava reflect our property's personality?

Her answers come from your factsheet — your wording for policies, amenities, and local recommendations — so the substance of every answer is yours.
