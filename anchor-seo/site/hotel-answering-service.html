A hotel answering service built for owner-operators.

Anchor is a hotel answering service powered by AI. It is designed for limited-service hotels that need reliable call coverage without adding another overnight employee or sending every missed call to voicemail.

After-hours hotel answering

Guests call after dark for rooms, late arrivals, directions, pet policy, breakfast hours, and local questions. Anchor answers those calls, uses the hotel's approved factsheet, and captures the details that staff need for follow-up.

Group and long-stay lead capture

Some calls are worth far more than one room night. Anchor can identify group block inquiries, sports teams, weddings, corporate travel, project crews, and long-stay guests, then send the lead to an owner or manager before it disappears.

Better than generic voicemail

Voicemail makes the caller wait and gives competitors time to win the booking. Anchor keeps the caller engaged, collects structured information, and routes the next action to the hotel. It is built for revenue capture, not just message taking.
