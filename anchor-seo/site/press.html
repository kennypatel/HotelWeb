Press

Anchor is a hotel AI phone agent built in Huntsville, Alabama by hotel owner-operators. It helps limited-service properties answer calls around the clock, capture group leads, and improve operational efficiency without PMS integration.

Company

Anchor serves limited-service hotel owners across brands and independent properties.

Why Anchor exists

Limited-service hotels lose revenue when calls go unanswered, especially after hours or during a busy front-desk shift. A traveler looking for a late room, a coach asking about a group block, or a long-stay guest comparing weekly options will often call the next property if nobody answers. Anchor was built to keep those conversations alive and turn them into clean follow-up for the hotel team.

Product summary

Anchor answers calls in real time, handles common guest questions, detects English and Spanish conversations, captures contact details, and routes high-value inquiries to the right hotel contact. The product works on top of the existing phone line and does not require direct integration with a property management system. That design helps hotels deploy faster and avoid unnecessary brand approval delays.

Audience

Anchor is built for owner-operators, general managers, and portfolio operators in the limited-service category. Typical use cases include after-hours hotel answering service, group sales capture, front desk overflow, guest FAQ handling, long-stay inquiry routing, and owner SMS alerts for revenue opportunities.

Location

The company is based in Huntsville, Alabama and is shaped by direct experience operating hotels in the region. Anchor’s public demo line lets prospects hear the voice agent before booking a sales call.
