Hotel AI that answers the phone when your front desk cannot.

Anchor is hotel AI built for limited-service hotels. Instead of a generic chatbot, Anchor works as an AI phone agent that answers guest calls, captures booking intent, handles common property questions, and routes high-value inquiries to hotel staff.

Why hotel AI matters

Hotels lose revenue when calls go unanswered. A guest looking for tonight's room, a coach asking about a group block, or a traveling worker comparing weekly stays will often call the next hotel if nobody picks up. Anchor keeps that demand from disappearing into voicemail.

Hotel AI use cases

Anchor can support after-hours hotel calls, front desk overflow, Spanish-language questions, late arrival details, parking and pet policy questions, group room blocks, corporate stays, long-stay inquiries, and owner alerts for valuable leads.

Built for hotel operators

Anchor was built by hotel owner-operators, so it is designed around how limited-service properties actually work. It sits on top of the existing phone line and does not require direct PMS integration or a long franchise approval process.
