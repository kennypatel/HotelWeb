# 12-Week SEO Execution Plan — Anchor for Hotels
Operating rhythm: outreach Mon–Wed, content Thu, follow-ups + tracking Fri. Cold-email volume ramps 10/day (wk 1–2) → 20/day (wk 3–4) → 30/day (wk 5+). Every send logged in `outreach_tracker.csv`; every live link logged in `backlink_tracker.csv`.

**Standing weekly metrics (log every Friday):**
- Referring domains (Search Console "Links" report + free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools)
- Indexed pages (Search Console coverage)
- Impressions + clicks for the 8 core keywords (GSC query filter)
- Outreach: sent / reply rate / accept rate
- Demo-line calls and demo requests (the metric that actually matters)

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

### Week 1 — Infrastructure & quick wins
- **Site:** Set up GA4, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools; submit sitemap.xml; implement Organization + Service JSON-LD sitewide (`technical_implementation.md`). Fix homepage title/meta per audit.
- **Publish:** the 3 finished landing pages (hotel-answering-service, overnight-hotel-call-answering, missed-call-recovery-hotels) + add footer Services links.
- **Directories (no outreach needed):** Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Crunchbase, LinkedIn company page audit, Dun & Bradstreet. (Copy: `local_seo_profiles.md`, `directory_submission_pack.md`.)
- **Prep:** finalize kenny@ outreach mailbox (SPF/DKIM/DMARC verified), logo/media kit per directory pack checklist.
- **LinkedIn:** posts 1–7 queued (plan days 1–7).

### Week 2 — Directory sprint A
- **Submissions (10–12):** G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Future Tools, Toolify, Product Hunt *prep* (don't launch yet), Hotel Tech Report vendor profile (the single most important listing — do it carefully).
- **Outreach (10/day):** template #3/#7/#8 emails where forms don't exist.
- **Content:** publish blog post 1 (idea #1: missed-call report) + blog post 2 (idea #22: mystery-call audit). These become link-target assets.
- **Local:** join Huntsville/Madison County Chamber; request member listing + new-member announcement.

### Week 3 — Directory sprint B + association push
- **Submissions (10–12):** remaining SaaS/AI/startup directories from CSV priority ≥ 60; Yelp, Alignable, BBB, chamberofcommerce.com.
- **Association outreach:** AAHOA allied membership inquiry (top vendor-relevance target), Alabama Restaurant & Hospitality Assn, NJ Restaurant & Hospitality Assn (template #9).
- **Content:** blog posts 3–4 (ideas #2 and #7).
- **Follow-ups:** Day-4/Day-10 bumps for week 2 sends.
- **Monitor:** first directory links going live → log in backlink tracker; check anchors match plan.

**Phase 1 exit targets:** 15+ referring domains, 8+ indexed pages, all profiles claimed, outreach machine running.

## Phase 2 — Outreach engine (Weeks 4–7)

**Phase 2 exit targets:** 35–50 referring domains, 2+ guest posts accepted, 2+ podcasts booked, 1+ press mention, all landing pages indexed.

## Phase 3 — Authority & compounding (Weeks 8–12)

### Week 8 — Original data asset
- **Create:** "The Hotel Missed-Call Report" — anonymized stats from Anchor's own call data (call-intent breakdown, after-hours volume %, Spanish-language %, group-lead frequency). Publish as a pillar page + downloadable PDF. This is your linkable asset for the rest of the year.
- **Pitch (15):** the report to every publication that declined a guest post ("no byline needed — cite the data").
- **Follow-ups + tracking.**

### Week 9 — Guest post wave 3 + association deepening
- Pitch ideas #14/#9 to Today's Hotelier, Asian Hospitality, Latino Hotel Assn (franchise + bilingual angles).
- AAHOA/state-association vendor listings should be completing — chase them.
- Blog 13–14 (start targeting comparison terms: "hotel answering service vs call center", "AI receptionist vs answering service").

### Week 10 — Podcast wave 2 + broken-link program
- Pitch 8 more podcasts with social proof from aired episodes.
- **Broken-link sprint:** run Screaming Frog or Check My Links across the top 30 hospitality resource pages in the CSV; send template #5 only where you have a true content match.
- Blog 15–16.

### Week 11 — Refresh & consolidation
- **On-page pass:** update titles/metas on any page with impressions but CTR < 1.5% (GSC data). Add internal links from every blog post to its cluster landing page.
- **Re-pitch:** the give-first variant to high-value non-responders.
- Blog 17–18.

### Week 12 — Review & next-quarter plan
- Full link audit: every row in backlink_tracker re-checked; disavow ONLY if something genuinely toxic appeared (rare; don't disavow preemptively).
- Scorecard vs. targets; double down on the two channels with best link-per-hour yield.
- Build Q2 plan: scale winning channel, add review-generation program on G2/Capterra/Hotel Tech Report (invite real customers through official flows only).

**Phase 3 exit targets:** 60–100 referring domains, 25+ indexed pages, 5+ published guest posts, 3+ podcast episodes live, top-10 rankings for ≥ 3 of the 8 core keywords, measurable demo-line call growth.

## Weekly follow-up discipline (every Friday, 60 minutes)
1. Filter outreach_tracker for sends 4 and 10 days old → send bumps.
2. Mark statuses; move 5 new prospects from CSV into the tracker for Monday.
3. Spot-check 10 oldest backlink rows for link removal/anchor changes.
4. Log the standing metrics; note one thing to stop doing and one to scale.
