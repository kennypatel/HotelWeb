Ava, Anchor's AI phone agent, answers calls 24/7 in English or Spanish: she handles common questions (breakfast hours, parking, pets, check-in, directions), gives starting-rate guidance, captures structured booking inquiries — including group blocks for sports teams, weddings, crews, and long stays — and transfers urgent or in-stay issues straight to staff.
