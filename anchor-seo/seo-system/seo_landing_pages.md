# SEO Landing Page Plan — Anchor for Hotels
14 pages forming the commercial cluster. Recommended structure: extension-less URLs at root (matches existing flat site) or under `/services/`. Every page: one H1, FAQ block with FAQPage schema, demo-line CTA above the fold, 900–1,400 words, 3+ internal links. Pages 1, 3, and 6 are fully written (see hotel-answering-service.md, overnight-hotel-call-answering.md, missed-call-recovery-hotels.md).

**Hub-and-spoke:** Page 1 (Hotel Answering Service) is the hub; all others link up to it; it links down to all of them.
