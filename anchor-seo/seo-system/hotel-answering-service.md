## Why generic answering services fail hotels

Most answering services were built for law offices and plumbers. They answer, take a name and number, and email you a message. For a hotel, that's a slow voicemail.

Hotel calls are different in three ways:

**They're perishable.** A caller asking "do you have a room tonight?" books within minutes — with you or the property down the road. A message in your inbox at 7am is a booking your competitor took at 11pm.

**They're layered.** The same line carries new bookings, group inquiries, current guests asking about checkout, vendors, and the occasional genuine emergency. Each needs different handling, and a generic operator with no property knowledge can't triage them.

**They carry rate risk.** An answering service that quotes rates wrong costs you money; one that refuses to discuss rates at all loses the caller. Ava gives approved starting-rate guidance — "our rates start at $110, depending on availability" — and routes the caller to your team for exact quotes and confirmation. You stay in control of every confirmed number.

## What Ava answers

- **Booking inquiries.** Availability questions, rate guidance, dates, party size, late check-ins — captured and routed for immediate follow-up, with same-night callers flagged.
- **Group and long-stay leads.** Sports tournaments, wedding blocks, construction crews, traveling nurses. Ava collects dates, room counts, contact details, and the reason for stay, then hands the lead to whoever does your group sales.
- **Guest FAQs.** Breakfast hours, parking, pets, pool, directions, check-in and checkout times — answered from a property factsheet you approve, so the answers are your answers.
- **Urgent transfers.** Lockouts, in-stay problems, emergencies, and manager requests go to your staff according to escalation rules you set. Ava never improvises on safety.

## Hotel answering service FAQs

**Does Anchor replace my front desk?**
No. Ava covers the phone so your desk can cover the lobby. In-person hospitality, confirmed bookings, sensitive situations, and final rate decisions stay with your team. Ava handles volume and after-hours coverage, and escalates what matters.

**Can it actually book the room?**
Ava captures the complete booking request — dates, room type, contact, special needs — and routes it for your team to confirm, typically with a callback in minutes during staffed hours. For many properties that's deliberately safer than giving an outside service booking authority; you keep control of rates and inventory.

**What happens with emergencies?**
Emergencies, lockouts, and in-stay issues transfer immediately to the on-call contact you designate. You define what counts as urgent, and those rules are tested before launch.

**Do you integrate with my PMS?**
Integration isn't required to start. Ava operates from your property factsheet and your phone line, which keeps setup fast and keeps you inside franchise rules. 

**What languages are supported?**
English and Spanish, automatically based on the caller — no transfers to a separate language line.

**How do I evaluate whether it's any good?**
Call the demo line: (256) 809-0866. Interrupt mid-sentence. Ask an oddball question. Ask for a manager. If Ava can't handle a normal caller, you'll know in ninety seconds.
