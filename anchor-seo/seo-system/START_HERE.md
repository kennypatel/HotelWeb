## Five non-negotiables (the white-hat guardrails baked into every file)
1. **Verify before you send.** Every contact/submission URL in the CSV is a best-known starting point — confirm it's live and find a named human before emailing. No scraped lists, ever.
2. **Personalize line one** of every outreach email or skip the prospect.
3. **Volume caps:** ≤30 emails/day, 2 follow-ups max, exclusive pitches (one outlet at a time per article).
4. **Anchor discipline:** 60% branded/naked, exact-match only in editorial placements, per anchor_text_strategy.md.
5. **Reviews only through official flows** (G2/Capterra/HTR invitations to real customers). No incentivized, no fake, no exceptions.
