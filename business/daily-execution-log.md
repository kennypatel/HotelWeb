## OBJECTION RESPONSE LOG

Track every objection encountered and the best response found:

| Objection | Response That Worked | Notes |
|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| "How do I know appointments will be qualified?" | Qualification criteria explanation + replacement guarantee | Works well |
| "Too busy right now" | "That's fine — can I follow up in 30 days?" | Keeps door open |
| "I already tried something like this" | "What happened? I'd love to understand what went wrong so I can avoid that." | Disarms with curiosity |
| "I need to think about it" | "What specifically would you need to feel confident?" | Forces them to articulate real objection |
| "What's your refund policy?" | "Cancel anytime. If I miss the appointment guarantee, I'll make up the shortfall." | |
