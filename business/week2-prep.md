## WHEN TO HIRE A VA (Virtual Assistant)

**Trigger:** You have 5+ clients and you're working 40+ hours/week.

**What to hire for:** Fulfillment only — lead research + sending outreach on behalf of clients. You stay on sales and quality control.

**Where to hire:** Onlinejobs.ph (Philippines-based VAs, excellent English, $400–$800/month)

**How to hand off:**
1. Record a Loom video walking through the SOP for one client
2. Have them shadow you for 3 sessions
3. They take over outreach sending and daily responses
4. You review all booked appointments before they're confirmed
5. You handle all client communication (weekly reports, calls)

This frees up 30 hours/week for you to focus on sales and growing to 20+ clients.
