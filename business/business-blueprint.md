## FULFILLMENT WORKFLOW

### For each client:
1. **Onboarding call (30 min)** — Get their ideal customer profile, service area, calendar link
2. **Build lead list** — Use Apollo.io (free), Google Maps, LinkedIn free search
3. **Write outreach** — Personalized cold email + LinkedIn DM (AI-assisted)
4. **Send outreach** — Gmail (free), 30–50 per day per client (within spam limits)
5. **Follow up** — Day 3 and Day 7 follow-up emails (templated)
6. **Handle responses** — Reply, qualify, and schedule via Calendly (free)
7. **Log everything** — Google Sheets CRM (free)
8. **Weekly report** — Send client a Google Sheet summary every Friday

### Tools (all free):
- **Lead sourcing:** Apollo.io free (50/month), Google Maps, LinkedIn
- **Email sending:** Gmail (up to 500/day free)
- **Scheduling:** Calendly free tier
- **CRM:** Google Sheets
- **Email warm-up:** Gmass free trial / manual warm-up
- **AI assistance:** Claude free tier (write copy, personalize)
- **Communication:** Slack free, Google Meet

### Time per client per week:
- Lead research: 2 hours
- Writing/sending outreach: 2 hours
- Responding + booking: 1 hour
- Reporting: 30 min
- **Total: ~5.5 hours/week per client**

At 6 clients: ~33 hours/week (manageable solo)

## CUSTOMER ONBOARDING PROCESS

**Day 1 (after payment):**
1. Send welcome email with onboarding form link (Google Form — free)
2. Schedule 30-min kick-off call via Calendly
3. Share Google Sheet pipeline tracker (read-only link for client)

**Onboarding Form captures:**
- Business name, website, phone
- Service area (city/radius)
- Top 3 services offered
- Ideal client description ("who is your best customer?")
- Average job/client value
- Calendar link (Google Calendar or Calendly)
- Anything they want to avoid in prospects

**Day 2–3:**
- Build first lead list (100 prospects in target area)
- Write first email sequence (3 emails: intro, follow-up 1, follow-up 2)
- Send for client review via email
- Once approved, begin sending

**Day 7:**
- First weekly report sent
- First round of follow-up emails sent
- Update pipeline tracker

**Day 14:**
- Check-in call (15 min) — first appointments should be booked by now
- Gather feedback, adjust targeting if needed

**Day 30:**
- Full month recap
- Invoice for Month 2
- Upsell conversation if they're happy

## DAILY EXECUTION PLAN (Solo Operator)

**Morning (9–10 AM): Outreach Block**
- Check responses from previous day's emails
- Reply to prospects, qualify, book appointments
- Update Google Sheets pipeline

**Mid-morning (10 AM–12 PM): Lead & Copy Block**
- Build new lead lists for active clients
- Write/personalize outreach for next send batch

**Afternoon (1–3 PM): Sales Block**
- Reach out to NEW potential clients (cold email + LinkedIn DM)
- Follow up on past AppointFlow sales outreach
- Check Facebook groups, Reddit, LinkedIn for warm leads

**End of Day (3–4 PM): Admin Block**
- Update pipeline tracking
- Answer client questions
- Prepare next day's send list

**Friday: Reports Block**
- Send weekly report to each client (Google Sheet link)
- Log what worked and what didn't
