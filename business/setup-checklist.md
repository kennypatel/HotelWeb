## CRITICAL: EMAIL WARM-UP PROTOCOL

**Week 1 sending limits — DO NOT EXCEED:**

| Day | Max Emails to Send | Cumulative |
|-----|--------------------|------------|
| Day 1 | 20 | 20 |
| Day 2 | 20 | 40 |
| Day 3 | 20 | 60 |
| Day 4 | 20 (follow-ups count) | 80 |
| Day 5 | 25 | 105 |
| Day 6 | 25 | 130 |
| Day 7 | 25 | 155 |

**Why this matters:** New Gmail accounts flagged for spam if they send 100+ emails/day immediately. Warm up slowly. Gmail's free limit is 500/day — but spam filters trigger on behavior pattern, not just volume.

**Signs of spam filter issues:**
- 0 replies across 20+ emails
- Bounce messages containing "550" or "spam" errors
- Very low open tracking (use Gmass free trial to check)

**Fix if suspected spam:**
- Stop sending for 48 hours
- Send 5–10 personal emails to real friends/contacts from the same account
- Switch to a plain text format only (no links in first email)
- Remove Calendly link from Email 1 (just ask for reply)
