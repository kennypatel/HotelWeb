## SALES CALL SCRIPT (Discovery Call — 15–20 min)

**Opening (2 min)**
"Thanks for taking the time. I'll keep this focused — I want to learn about your business first, then show you exactly what we do and whether it makes sense. Does that work?"

**Discovery (5 min)**
1. "How do you currently get most of your new clients?" (Listen — referrals, ads, walk-ins, etc.)
2. "What would a good month look like — how many new jobs/clients would you want?" (Get a number)
3. "And what's the average value of a new customer for you?" (This unlocks ROI math)
4. "What's the biggest frustration with your current pipeline — is it volume, quality, consistency?"

**The Pain Amplifier (2 min)**
"So if I'm hearing you right — right now you're mainly relying on [referrals/whatever they said], and the challenge is that it's not predictable. Some months are great and others are slow. Is that fair?"

[Let them confirm.]

"And in a slow month, what does that actually cost you — not just in revenue but in stress, paying your team, etc.?"

[Let them feel the pain.]

**The Offer (3 min)**
"What we do at AppointFlow is take all of that off your plate. I personally reach out to [X type of prospects] in [their city], qualify them, and book them onto your calendar — so you just show up and close.

You don't run ads. You don't make cold calls. You don't chase leads. You just talk to people who are already interested.

Our standard plan starts at $500/month, and we guarantee 10 qualified appointments. At your average job value of $[X], you only need to close [1–2] of them to more than pay for the service.

But since we haven't worked together before, I want to earn your trust first. So here's what I'd like to do: I'll book your first 3 appointments completely free. Zero cost. If you like the quality and want to continue, we move to the paid plan."

**Handling Objections:**

*"How do you know they'll be qualified?"*
"Great question. Before I book anything, I qualify them — meaning they're in your service area, they have an actual need for [service], and they're willing to schedule a conversation with you. You won't get tire-kickers."

*"What if the appointments don't show?"*
"We send a confirmation and a reminder. Our show rate is above 80%. But if anyone no-shows, we replace the appointment."

*"I'm already busy."*
"That's actually perfect — it means your operation works. The question is: when you have capacity again, do you have a consistent pipeline ready to fill it? We just make sure the answer is always yes."

*"I need to think about it."*
"Totally fair. Can I ask — what specifically would you need to see or know to feel confident moving forward? Let me address that now so you're not left wondering."

**Close (2 min)**
"So here's what I'd recommend: let me start your free trial this week. I'll build a list of [type of prospect] in [city], we'll agree on the messaging, and I'll start reaching out by [specific day].

Does that sound good? I'll send over a quick onboarding form right after this call — takes 5 minutes — and we'll be live within 48 hours."

**If they say yes:** "Perfect. I'll send the onboarding form and a calendar link for a 30-minute kick-off call to [email]. Looking forward to filling your calendar."

## LEAD LIST CRITERIA (for finding AppointFlow's own clients)

### Source 1: Google Maps
- Search: "[niche] in [city]"
- Filter: 3–4 star rated (hungry for more business)
- Collect: Business name, owner name, email (from website), phone
- Volume: 50–100 per city per niche

### Source 2: Apollo.io (Free Tier — 50 exports/month)
- Job titles: "Owner," "Founder," "CEO," "General Manager"
- Industry: Plumbing, HVAC, Roofing, Dental Practice, Real Estate
- Company size: 1–20 employees
- Geography: Target city
- Email available: Yes (filter for verified emails only)

### Source 3: LinkedIn (Free Search)
- Search: "[niche] owner [city]"
- Filter: 1st and 2nd degree connections
- Collect: Name, headline, company
- Note: Cannot export directly — manual list building

### Source 4: Facebook Groups
- Search: "[niche] business owners [state/city]"
- Join 5–10 relevant groups
- Identify active members who post about business challenges

### Qualification criteria before outreach:
- Has a real business (active Google Maps listing or website)
- Serving a local area (not national chains — too slow to decide)
- Clear service business (not pure e-commerce or product)
- Owner-operated or small team (decision maker is reachable)
- Not already using an appointment setting service (check their website/LinkedIn)

## EMAIL ACCOUNT SETUP (to avoid spam filters)

1. Create Gmail account: appointflow@gmail.com (or similar)
2. Set up Google Workspace free trial ($0 for 14 days, then $6/month — delay until revenue)
3. Send max 50 emails/day for first 2 weeks (warm-up period)
4. Increase to 100/day in week 3, 150/day in week 4
5. Always use plain text emails — no HTML, no images, no attachments
6. Personalize first line of every email (mention their city/business name)
7. Include unsubscribe option: "Reply STOP to be removed"
8. Never use spam trigger words: "free money," "guaranteed," "limited time," "act now"
