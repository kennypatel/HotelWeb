FAQ

Common questions.

What do you mean by "qualified" appointments?

Before we book anyone on your calendar, we confirm: they're in your service area, they have a genuine need for your service in the near term, and they're willing to have a real conversation with you. We don't book tire-kickers or people who are "just browsing."

What if someone doesn't show up?

We send confirmation and reminder messages to every booked prospect. Our show rate is consistently above 80%. If anyone no-shows, we replace that appointment at no extra charge.

How quickly will I get my first appointment?

Most clients see their first booked appointment within 5–10 business days of onboarding. Some get their first booking within 48 hours. Results depend on your market and niche — we'll be upfront with you during the discovery call.

Do I need to do anything?

Just two things: complete our 5-minute onboarding form and show up for your booked appointments. We handle every single step in between.

What happens during the free trial?

We book your first 3 appointments completely free. No credit card required. After that, if you're happy with the quality and want to continue, you choose a monthly plan. If not, you owe nothing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month on all plans. No contracts, no cancellation fees. We keep you by delivering results — not by locking you in.
